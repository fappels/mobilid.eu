Go into menu Home - Setup - Modules - Deploy an external module and submit the module zip file.
Module 'SHIPMENT PACKAGES' is visible in list of modules (CRM Section) and can be activated and configured.

Features
Smart configuration - Decide how your package value is calculated for transport insurance (based on WAP or sell price), choose the numbering scheme that fits your workflow, and enable ready-to-print A4 content sheets and/or A6 barcode labels in just a few clicks.
Create packages in one click - Turn any validated shipment into a package instantly, group several shipments from the same customer into a single package, and find every package you've created neatly listed in the shipment's linked objects.
Standalone packaging - Prefer to start from scratch? Pick your products, set the quantities to ship, and build a package with no shipment required.
Full visibility - Access every package you've ever created from one centralized list, always at your fingertips.
All the details that matter - Link a project and shipping method, flag dangerous goods or a tail lift requirement, choose a parcel type, and let the weight be calculated automatically from your product data. Add your shipping supplier and their tracking number, printed automatically as a barcode on the A6 label.
Seamlessly integrated - Every package comes with Dolibarr's familiar contacts, notes, linked files and agenda tabs, so it fits right into your existing workflow.

Please check also our other modules Mobilid, Block Outstanding and Backorder.
